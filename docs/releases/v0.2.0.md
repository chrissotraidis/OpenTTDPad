# OpenTTDPad 0.2.0: first public preview

OpenTTDPad is an unofficial iPadOS port of [OpenTTD](https://www.openttd.org/) 15.3
with touch-first construction, a precision loupe, Apple Pencil, trackpad and
keyboard support, desktop-compatible saves, and free graphics, sound and music.
Nothing else is needed to play: no original Transport Tycoon Deluxe data, no
account and no network connection.

## Highlights

- **iPadOS 27 support.** Earlier test builds opened to a black screen on iPadOS 27.
  The game window now belongs to the app's UIKit scene, using the same SDL 2.32.10
  scene backport as the other Pad ports.
- **External displays.** The game window follows its scene when it rotates,
  resizes or moves to another display, and adopts that screen's pixel density.
- **Touch that understands OpenTTD.** Tap, drag to build, pinch to zoom,
  two-finger pan and tap, touch-and-hold precision loupe, 44-point touch targets,
  a Cancel button for construction, and left- or right-handed control placement.
- **Free content built in.** OpenGFX 8.0, OpenSFX 1.0.3, OpenMSX 0.4.2 and
  TimGM6mb MIDI music.
- **Files and saves.** Import saves and content from Files; export all saves as
  a ZIP. Saves are OpenTTD 15.3 saves and open on desktop OpenTTD.
- **Online Content** over verified HTTPS, and international text through
  FreeType, HarfBuzz, ICU and CoreText.

## Polish from hands-on testing

- **Taps land where your finger lands.** A press held a little longer than a
  quick tap used to be ignored, and the click was taken where the finger lifted.
  Small targets such as window close boxes now respond to every press.
- **The precision loupe shows exactly the spot it will tap.** It was looking
  twice as far above the finger as the tap it committed.
- **Tidier controls.** The ⋯ menu sits just under the toolbar's Help button,
  opens downward, and steps below a construction toolbar docked in that corner.
  Cancel is a compact pill above the status bar instead of a large red block.
- **About OpenTTDPad** replaces the plain licenses screen: version, links to the
  source, issues and PadMint, credits, and the full license texts.
- **The OpenTTD logo is the app icon**, on a navy tile-grid backdrop, so the
  game is easy to recognise on the Home Screen.
- **Better crash reports.** OpenTTD's crash logs on iPad now include a stack
  trace and the reason for any uncaught exception.

## Install

Choose one:

- **Download** `OpenTTDPad-unsigned.ipa` below and install it with a sideloading
  tool that signs it with your Apple ID, such as Sideloadly or AltStore, or sign
  it yourself with Xcode or `devicectl`. With a free Apple ID, refresh or
  reinstall it every seven days; your saves stay.
- **Build it with [PadMint](https://github.com/chrissotraidis/padmint)** on an
  Apple Silicon Mac with Xcode 26 or newer.
- **Build from source:** see [Get OpenTTDPad](https://github.com/chrissotraidis/OpenTTDPad#get-openttdpad).

Requires an iPad on iPadOS 17 or later, in landscape. iPhone is not supported.
Installing needs about 65 MB of free space.

The bundle ID is now `com.chrissotraidis.openttdpad`. A private test copy
installed before this release (`org.openttd.OpenTTDPad`) stays as a separate
app; export its saves from the options menu before deleting it.

Found a problem? Use the [bug report form](https://github.com/chrissotraidis/OpenTTDPad/issues/new/choose).
Crash logs are in Files → On My iPad → OpenTTDPad → OpenTTD.

## What has been checked

- Physical iPad Pro 12.9-inch (6th generation), iPadOS 27.2.1: installs over an
  existing copy, launches to the title screen at full screen, plays music, and
  accepts touch.
- XCTest UI suite, 21/21 on iOS 27.0 Simulators (iPad Pro 13-inch, iPad, iPad mini):
  touch construction, cancellation, settings, save export and background/foreground.

Not yet checked on hardware: a complete touch-only game, save/force-quit/reload,
Pencil, trackpad, keyboard, an external monitor or Stage Manager, Bluetooth
audio, heat and long sessions. Reports from real iPads are the most useful
contribution right now.
